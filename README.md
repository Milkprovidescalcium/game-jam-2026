# Signal: A Co-Op Puzzle Game

About
-----

Signal is a fast paced two player co-op puzzle game where you will play the role of ground control and a crew member to ensure the smooth operation of a space ship.

Created in 30 hours for Umanitoba CSSA Game Jam 2026 you must work together with a teammate to solve tasks and complete repairs on the ship. It's a race against the clock for you and your partner as you run around fixing stuff trying not to let the ship freaking explode!!!

Controls
--------

Point and click with cursor on elements on the screen to expand them and press `ESC` to exit the view.
